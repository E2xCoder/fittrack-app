# FitTrack

Nutrition and fitness tracking in one place: log meals and workouts, hit your calorie, macro and fiber targets, and see your progress over time.

**Live:** https://fittrackme.com

## What it does

- Meal logging with a food database, barcode scanning and reusable meal packs
- Calorie, protein, carb, fat and fiber tracking against your daily targets
- Workout logging by split, with set-by-set tracking
- Weekly meal planning
- Body and progress tracking
- Sign in with email, Google or passkey, with optional 2FA

## Built with

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Better Auth, Recharts
